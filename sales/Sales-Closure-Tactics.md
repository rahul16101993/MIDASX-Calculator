## 0. The MIDASX closing philosophy

Three rules the whole team operates under.

1. **The calculator is the negotiator, not you.** Quote from the in-product line: *"Coffee is for closers. Discounts are for calculators."* The pricing calculator at `pricing.html` is non-negotiable RM-side. Any discount above 10% requires a written internal note. The calculator is what makes price feel *fair* — you don't argue, you compute.
2. **You don't sell features. You sell hours saved, deals not lost, and the version of their practice they want to be in 18 months.** Features are evidence. The story is the close.
3. **No close on the first call.** First call is discovery + diagnosis. Close on call 2 or 3 with a personalised quote PDF from the calculator. This works for both Arjun and Priya — they need to feel they were *consulted with*, not *sold to*.

---

## 1. The 5-stage MIDASX sales cycle

Every deal goes through these stages. The tactics in sections 2 and 3 plug into this skeleton.

| Stage | Goal | Duration | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1 — Trigger / Inbound** | Get on a 30-min discovery call | 0-3 days | Calendar invite |
| **S2 — Discovery** | Diagnose pain in their own words; map persona | 30 min | Pain inventory + persona tag |
| **S3 — Tailored Demo** | Show 3 things — no more — that hit their top 3 pains | 45 min | "Wow moment" + commercial ask |
| **S4 — Quote + Champion Build** | Send PDF quote from calculator + 1-pager ROI | 1-5 days | Internal champion to push it through |
| **S5 — Close** | Negotiate, paper, kick-off | 2-7 days | Signed MSA + onboarding kickoff |

**Hard rule:** if a deal sits in S4 > 14 days, it's not stuck on price — it's stuck on champion. Go find the champion.

## 2. Persona 1 — Arjun (Digital-Native New MFD) → AI Only ₹25k

### 2.1 Where you find him
- Instagram / YouTube / X comments on personal finance reels
- AMFI new ARN list (last 24 months)
- BSE Star MF onboarding webinars (he attends)
- Referrals from existing Pro / Pro+AI customers ("who's the youngest MFD you know?")
- LinkedIn — title "Mutual Fund Distributor" + content frequency >2/week

### 2.2 Discovery call (30 min) — the 7 questions that diagnose him

Run these in order. Listen for the words in **bold** — those are buying signals.

1. **"Walk me through how you got your first 50 investors."**
   → Listen for: **content**, **WhatsApp**, **Instagram**. Confirms persona.
2. **"How many investors today, and what's your average ticket?"**
   → Need: investor count >50, avg ticket <₹50k. If above, he's actually Priya.
3. **"How much of your week goes to content vs operations?"**
   → If ops > content, the platform sells itself. If content > ops, sell ahead of pain.
4. **"What's the one thing that's slowing you down right now?"**
   → His exact words go into the quote PDF later. Write them down verbatim.
5. **"Show me how you send a portfolio review today."** (have him share screen)
   → You'll watch him fumble through CAMS/KFin PDFs. This is your demo hook.
6. **"What tools are you paying for today, and what's the monthly burn?"**
   → Anchor: if he's already paying ₹3-5k/month in scattered tools, ₹25k/yr (₹2,083/mo) is *cheaper than his current chaos*.
7. **"What would you build in your practice if you had 2 free hours a day?"**
   → His answer becomes your future-pacing close.

### 2.3 Demo flow (45 min) — the three "wow moments" only

Do not show him CRM screens. Show:

1. **The Avatar Wow (10 min)** — Generate a 30-second video portfolio statement with his face + voice template + branded background. Send it to *his own WhatsApp* during the call. This is the moment he forwards it to a peer. Conversion lifts ~30% when this happens live.
2. **The AI Wow (10 min)** — Ask Portfolio Genius AI to draft a portfolio review for a sample HNI investor. Then ask the AI chat assistant to write 5 Instagram reel hooks on tax-saving funds. He will mentally calculate how many hours/week this saves.
3. **The Unit Economics Wow (10 min)** — Open `pricing.html` live. Show: AI Only at ₹25k = ₹125/investor/year if he has 200 investors. Frame it as: *"You charge nothing for advisory. ₹125 a year per investor to look like a fintech is rounding error on your trail."*

Last 15 minutes: silence. Let him talk. Take notes. Do NOT pitch.

### 2.4 Objection handling — exact scripts

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| **"₹25k is still a lot for me."** | *"It is — until you compare it to one HNI you'll lose this year because your portfolio statements look like everyone else's. One investor with ₹10L AUM at 0.6% trail pays for MIDASX for a decade. The question isn't ₹25k. The question is whether you'd rather pay yourself ₹25k for content time or pay it to look the same as every other new MFD."* |
| **"Will I get more features as I grow?"** | *"You get a clear upgrade path. AI Only today, Pro+AI when you cross 150 investors, Enterprise when you cross ₹100 Cr. We hold your data across upgrades — you don't migrate, you unlock."* |
| **"Will this work for small ticket SIPs?"** | *"That's exactly who it's built for. High investor count, low ticket — the operational pain only platforms solve. The AMC tools assume one investor at a time."* |
| **"Can I cancel anytime?"** | *"It's annual, but if you cancel within 30 days you get a pro-rata refund. That's not in the contract because we made it a rule for new MFDs specifically. We don't want to be in your life if we're not earning it."* |
| **"My competitor uses [free tool] — why pay?"** | *"Show me his Instagram. Show me yours. Now ask yourself why you're the one researching paid platforms and he's not."* |

### 2.5 The close — three techniques that work on Arjun

- **Technique 1 — The "Send-It-to-Yourself" close.** End the demo by generating a portfolio statement video with HIS face, send it to his WhatsApp, then say: *"That's the first video you'll send a client this week. The platform pays for itself the day you do."*
- **Technique 2 — The "Cohort" close.** *"We're onboarding 12 new MFDs this month. Three of them are in your city. I can lock you in this week or you can subscribe in three months when one of them eats your lunch."* (Only use if true. Don't lie about cohorts.)
- **Technique 3 — The "120-day milestone" close.** *"Sign today, and if in 120 days you haven't added 25 investors or saved 5 hours a week, I will personally process your refund. That's not a contract clause. That's me on WhatsApp."*

### 2.6 Pricing anchors and discount discipline

- **List price:** ₹25,000/yr
- **Floor:** ₹22,500/yr (10% — needs no approval)
- **Below floor:** Don't. Instead, give him 3 free months of **Digital Avatar add-on** (an extras add-on, not a discount on base).
- **Never:** Bundle Pro features into AI Only as a "trial." It rewires expectations and kills the upgrade path.

### 2.7 Expected economics
- Average cycle: 9 days
- Win rate from S2 → close: 35-45%
- Year-1 ACV: ₹25-28k
- 18-month upgrade rate (AI Only → Pro+AI): ~40% if he survives
- Churn risk: highest in first 6 months — onboarding usage gates matter

## 3. Persona 2 — Priya (Ambitious Growth MFD) → Pro+AI ₹75k

### 3.1 Where you find her
- LinkedIn — title "Mutual Fund Distributor / Founder / RIA" + posts about RIA conversion, advisory fees, or scaling
- Existing customer referrals (highest converting channel for her)
- AMFI Top 1000 ARN list, growth >30% YoY
- BSE Star MF / MFU partner webinars
- Vijya Fintech newsletter subscribers who clicked >2 emails
- CFP/RIA workshops, NISM events

### 3.2 Discovery call (30 min) — the 7 questions that diagnose her

1. **"What's your AUM today and what's the number you want to hit in 18 months?"**
   → If current is ₹25-120 Cr and target doubles it, she's in.
2. **"How long does it take to onboard a new investor today, from first call to first SIP?"**
   → Her answer (often 2-5 days) is the demo anchor.
3. **"Are you charging advisory fees? How are you billing and collecting them?"**
   → If yes-but-messy: massive opening. If no-but-thinking: even bigger opening.
4. **"How many tools is your team using right now?"**
   → List them. The list is the case. Count tools, count subscription costs, compute.
5. **"What's the last deal you lost, and why?"**
   → Her HNI loss story is gold. Mirror it back during the demo.
6. **"Who else looks at your practice's numbers — partner, spouse, CA, no one?"**
   → Identifies who else has to say yes. You need that name.
7. **"In a perfect world, what does your Monday morning look like 12 months from now?"**
   → Future-pacing. Her answer is the script for the close.

### 3.3 Demo flow (45 min) — three precision strikes, not a tour

Do NOT show her the full feature list. Show:

1. **The 5-Minute Onboarding (15 min)** — Live: from a prospect's PAN, through DigiLocker DIY journey, e-sign, risk profile, model portfolio assignment, first SIP setup. Time it on a stopwatch. Her current process: 2-5 days. Yours: 5 minutes. Don't editorialise — the stopwatch is the close.
2. **The Advisory Fee Story (15 min)** — Live: create an advisory fee plan, tag it to a client, generate a GST invoice, trigger collection via WhatsApp. Then show the receivable dashboard. She has wanted this for 2 years.
3. **The Weekend-Back Story (10 min)** — Portfolio Genius AI generates portfolio reviews for her top 20 HNIs in 90 seconds. Then AI chat assistant drafts personalised WhatsApp messages for each. Frame: *"This is what you're doing on Sunday morning. Now it's done by Sunday 10am."*

Last 5 minutes: open `pricing.html`, configure Pro+AI with her exact investor count, generate the quote PDF, send it to her email **during the call**. She must see her name on the PDF before she hangs up.

### 3.4 Objection handling — exact scripts

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| **"Why not [free AMC portal] + [free CRM]?"** | *"Because you already are using them, and you're still spending weekends on portfolio reviews. The free stack is what got you to ₹50 Cr. It's not what gets you to ₹200 Cr. What's missing isn't features — it's that they don't talk to each other. That's the entire reason MIDASX exists."* |
| **"₹75k is a lot right now."** | *"Let's do the math together."* (Open the calculator. Plug in HER investor count and HER advisory fee target.) *"If you charge ₹15,000/year advisory to just 5 clients, the platform is free. The first 5 are the cost. Everything after that is margin."* |
| **"I need ROI in 3 months."** | *"Here's how it pays back in 90 days: (1) Onboarding time drops from 3 days to 5 minutes → you take 8 more clients this quarter you'd otherwise lose. (2) Advisory fee collection becomes systematic → if you have ₹50k of uncollected fees today, this finds and bills them. (3) Weekend portfolio reviews → 8 weekends, 4 hours each = 32 hours back. At your hourly value of work, that's the ₹75k by itself."* |
| **"Does it work with my existing tools?"** | *"Tell me your stack — let's check item by item."* Have the integrations list ready: CAMS/KFin, BSE Star MF, MFU, MF Central, WhatsApp Business API, Google Calendar, Zoho/Sanchay CRM, DigiLocker. If she names one we don't support, *don't lie* — say so, then offer to add it to roadmap. Honesty closes Priya. |
| **"Let me think about it."** | *"Of course — what specifically are you thinking about? Price, fit, timing, or someone else's opinion?"* Then handle whichever she names. "Let me think" is never the real objection. |
| **"I want to talk to my [partner / spouse / CA]."** | *"Smart. Let's get them on a 20-min call together — Tuesday or Thursday?"* Never let the second meeting be without you in the room. |
| **"Send me a proposal."** | *"Already in your inbox — sent during this call. Open it now while I'm here so we can walk through it."* |

### 3.5 The close — three techniques that work on Priya

- **Technique 1 — The Calculator Close.** *"You configure the plan in the calculator yourself — I'll just sit here. Tell me when the number on screen feels right."* Hand her control. The calculator gives her power; you give her permission. Conversion lifts because *she* chose it.
- **Technique 2 — The "What does Q4 look like?" close.** Get her to describe — out loud — what her practice looks like 12 months from now with MIDASX. The future-pacing she did in discovery comes back here. Then ask: *"What's between you and that picture?"* The answer is almost always the signature.
- **Technique 3 — The Champion Recruit close.** If she has a partner / spouse / CA, *bring them in by name.* *"What would Rohit need to see to say yes?"* — then build the proposal around Rohit. She closes when her champion closes.
- **Bonus — The "First-100" co-marketing close.** *"You're in our first 100 Pro+AI cohort. We'll co-market your case study — your face on our landing page, our team on your YouTube channel."* (Only if true — and capped at 100.)

### 3.6 Pricing anchors and discount discipline

- **List price:** ₹75,000/yr (Pro+AI)
- **Floor:** ₹67,500/yr (10% — RM approval not required)
- **Annual prepay discount:** Already baked in to ₹75k. No extra annual discount.
- **2-year prepay:** 12% off year-2 (₹66k) — needs Sales Head approval. Use when she asks for "your best price."
- **Add-on lever (use before discount):** Throw in **6 months of API access** or **3 months of Digital Avatar** to land the deal at list. Adding value beats cutting price every time on this persona.
- **Hard "no":** No discount that crosses 15% without written CFO/Founder approval. Priya respects the discipline — it signals you're not a vendor that caves.

### 3.7 Expected economics
- Average cycle: 21-28 days
- Win rate from S2 → close: 25-35%
- Year-1 ACV: ₹70-80k (sometimes higher with add-ons)
- 18-month upgrade rate (Pro+AI → Enterprise): ~15% (the ones that scale past ₹150 Cr)
- Churn risk: low — once she has 90 days of usage, switching cost is real
- Referral rate: highest of any persona — Priya talks about her stack publicly

## 4. Cross-persona — what's the same

### 4.1 Discovery → demo → close cadence
| Persona | S2 to S3 | S3 to S4 | S4 to S5 (close) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arjun | 1-2 days | Same call | 2-5 days |
| Priya | 3-5 days | 3-7 days | 7-14 days |

### 4.2 The "do not do" list (both personas)

- **Do not** open with a product demo. Discovery first, always.
- **Do not** send a price by email without a call. Calculator-PDF only, with you on a call.
- **Do not** over-discount. Every 1% off list is 1% you can't get back at renewal.
- **Do not** show features that don't solve their stated pain. Discipline > completeness.
- **Do not** chase. After 2 follow-ups with no response, send a single "closing the loop" note and stop. Half of these come back in 60-90 days.
- **Do not** badmouth competitors by name. Mirror their tagline back as a question: *"What does their AI actually do that mine doesn't?"*

### 4.3 The 4 leading indicators that say "this deal will close"

Watch for any of these — when 2 of 4 happen, the deal is yours to lose:

1. They forward your demo recording or PDF quote internally.
2. They use the word "we" instead of "I" when describing using MIDASX.
3. They start asking about *onboarding* (not features, not price).
4. They share screen unprompted to show you their current mess.
