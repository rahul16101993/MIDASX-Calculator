# MIDASX — MFD Buyer Personas (Draft v1)

> Working document for sales playbook. These personas are reconstructed from the
> Indian MFD market segmentation and the MIDASX feature ladder (AI-Only ₹25k →
> Pro ₹50k → Pro+AI ₹75k → Enterprise ₹5L+). Confirm / correct before we layer
> closure tactics on top.

---

## How to use this document

Each persona has the same shape so the sales team can map a prospect in <60 seconds on a discovery call:

1. **Snapshot** — who they are at a glance
2. **Book of business** — AUM, investor count, ARN structure, geography
3. **A day in their life** — what their workflow actually looks like
4. **Pain points** — the things that wake them up at 2am
5. **What they say they want** vs **what they actually buy on**
6. **Buying triggers** — events that flip them from "thinking" to "signing"
7. **Objections you will hear**
8. **Best-fit MIDASX plan** and the 2-3 features that close the deal
9. **Disqualifiers** — when to walk away

## Persona 1 — "Suresh the Solo Veteran"

### Snapshot
Individual ARN holder, 45-58 years old, second-generation distributor or ex-LIC/bank RM who turned MFD 10-20 years ago. Tier-2/3 city (Indore, Nashik, Coimbatore, Lucknow). Runs the practice from a single office with 1-2 staff, often a family member.

### Book of business
- **AUM:** ₹40 Cr – ₹200 Cr
- **Investors:** 200 – 600 families (mostly multi-generational)
- **ARN structure:** Single individual ARN
- **Geography:** One city, occasional travel for HNI clients
- **Revenue mix:** ~85% MF trail, 10% insurance, 5% FD/bonds

### A day in their life
- Morning: BSE Star MF / MFU portal for transactions
- Afternoon: WhatsApp calls, branch visits, family meetings at client homes
- Excel-heavy. Trail receivable reconciled manually monthly. Capital gains and IT reports outsourced to a CA in March/April.
- CRM = a diary + WhatsApp pinned chats.

### Pain points
- "I'm losing 2-3 hours a day to portfolio statements and queries that should be automated."
- AUM reconciliation against RTA feeds = monthly nightmare.
- Children/successors won't join a paper-based practice.
- Younger investors expect an app — he doesn't have one.
- Compliance load (BASL, KYC refresh, nominee updates) is growing.

### What they say they want vs what they actually buy on
- **Says:** "I want a simple app for my clients."
- **Buys on:** Saving 2 hours/day, looking professional vs the competing IFA next door, and a successor-ready digital practice.

### Buying triggers
- Lost an HNI to a fintech RIA who showed a slick app.
- Son/daughter joining the practice and pushing for digitisation.
- RTA reconciliation error that cost trail commission.
- SEBI/AMFI compliance circular he can't fulfil with Excel.

### Objections you will hear
- "₹50,000 a year is too much — my AMC gives me free tools."
- "My clients are 60+, they don't use apps."
- "I tried [competitor] before, it was too complex."
- "Let me think about it / let me discuss with my son."

### Best-fit plan
**Pro ₹50k/yr** — and the closing features are:
1. **MF Central + RTA import** (kills the reconciliation pain)
2. **Client mobile app + WhatsApp integration** (the "looking modern" win)
3. **Capital gains + IT report automation** (saves the CA fee and March stress)

Upsell path: Add **Digital Avatar** add-on for birthday/anniversary videos — Suresh loves the personal touch angle.

### Disqualifiers
- AUM < ₹20 Cr — unit economics don't work for him.
- Refuses to give up paper after a 30-min demo — you're fighting psychology, not price.

## Persona 2 — "Priya the Ambitious Growth MFD"

### Snapshot
30-42 years old, NISM-certified 5-10 years ago, often a career switcher (ex-banker, ex-CA, ex-corporate). Metro or Tier-1 city. Building a brand, active on LinkedIn/YouTube, may run a small team of 2-4.

### Book of business
- **AUM:** ₹25 Cr – ₹120 Cr
- **Investors:** 150 – 500
- **ARN structure:** Individual ARN, exploring partnership / corporate ARN
- **Geography:** Metro-led, pan-India clients via digital
- **Revenue mix:** 70% MF trail, 15% advisory fee (RIA-curious), 10% PMS/AIF referrals, 5% insurance

### A day in their life
- Juggling 5-6 tools: BSE Star MF, MFU, an Excel CRM, Canva, a free email tool, Calendly.
- Records reels and shorts twice a week.
- Onboards 3-5 new investors a month, mostly inbound from content.
- Pitches SIPs, goal-based plans, and is starting to charge advisory fees.

### Pain points
- Tool sprawl — nothing talks to anything else.
- Manual onboarding takes 45-60 mins per investor; loses leads.
- Wants to charge advisory fees but has no clean way to invoice + collect + tag.
- "Looking small" when competing with a fintech RIA on a HNI prospect.
- Spends weekends on portfolio reviews instead of content.

### What they say they want vs what they actually buy on
- **Says:** "I want one platform that handles everything."
- **Buys on:** Time-to-onboard a new investor (from 45 min → 5 min), the ability to look enterprise-grade on day one, and AI assist on portfolio reviews so weekends are free.

### Buying triggers
- Crossed ₹50 Cr AUM — feels she's outgrown spreadsheets.
- Lost a HNI deal because onboarding took 3 days.
- Started charging advisory fees and realised collection is a mess.
- Saw a peer demo a robo-advisory journey on LinkedIn.

### Objections you will hear
- "Why not just use [free AMC portal] + [free CRM]?"
- "₹75k for Pro+AI is a lot for me right now."
- "I need to see ROI in 3 months."
- "Does it work with [her existing tool stack]?"

### Best-fit plan
**Pro + AI ₹75k/yr** — closing features:
1. **Digital partner onboarding with e-sign + DigiLocker DIY journey** (the 5-min onboarding story)
2. **Advisor fee administration — creation, tagging, invoicing + collection** (the RIA-ready story)
3. **Portfolio Genius AI + AI chat assistant** (her weekend-back story)
4. **Robo advisory end-to-end** (the "I look like a fintech" story)

Cross-sell: **API access** add-on once she builds her own landing page funnel.

### Disqualifiers
- Not yet willing to pay for tools — still in "free tier collector" mindset. Nurture, don't push.
- AUM < ₹15 Cr with no growth plan — wait 6 months.

## Persona 3 — "Arjun the Digital-Native New MFD"

### Snapshot
24-32 years old, NISM/CFP cleared in last 1-3 years. Often a content creator first, MFD second. Active on Instagram/YouTube/Twitter. Single-handedly runs the practice from home/coworking.

### Book of business
- **AUM:** ₹2 Cr – ₹30 Cr
- **Investors:** 50 – 250 (high count, low ticket size)
- **ARN structure:** Fresh individual ARN
- **Geography:** Pan-India, mostly online-acquired
- **Revenue mix:** 95% MF trail (lots of SIPs), 5% misc

### A day in their life
- Records reels, replies to DMs, posts shorts.
- Lives inside BSE Star MF for transactions.
- Investor count is high but tickets are small — operational load disproportionate to revenue.
- Talks to investors via WhatsApp groups and broadcast lists.

### Pain points
- 200 small investors, no scalable way to send statements, reviews, or nudges.
- Can't afford ₹50k+ tools — but knows they'd help.
- Manual everything = burnout incoming.
- Wants to look credible vs incumbents on his Instagram.

### What they say they want vs what they actually buy on
- **Says:** "Cheapest plan that has AI."
- **Buys on:** Per-investor unit economics, branded video portfolio statements he can share on WhatsApp, and AI that 10x's his content creation.

### Buying triggers
- Hit 100 investors and broke under operational load.
- Saw a competitor share an AI-generated portfolio review video.
- Onboarded his first HNI ticket (₹25L+) — suddenly needs to look professional.

### Objections you will hear
- "₹25k is still a lot for me."
- "Will I get more features as I grow?"
- "Will this work for very small ticket SIPs?"
- "Can I cancel anytime?"

### Best-fit plan
**AI Only ₹25k/yr** — closing features:
1. **AI chat assistant + ChatGPT integration** (content + research)
2. **Digital Avatar — 500 videos/30s each** (the WhatsApp shareable wow factor)
3. **Portfolio Genius AI** (reviews at scale)

Growth path: Move to **Pro+AI** when he crosses 150 investors. Sell the upgrade with usage data.

### Disqualifiers
- Hasn't crossed 30 investors yet — even ₹25k won't pay back. Offer a wait-and-watch + free educational content path.

## Persona 4 — "The Mehta Family Firm" (Multi-ARN Established Practice)

### Snapshot
LLP / partnership / private limited, 2-4 ARN holders (usually family), employs 5-15 staff including paraplanners and ops. 15-30 year vintage. Tier-1 city with branches/satellite offices. Diversified beyond MF.

### Book of business
- **AUM:** ₹150 Cr – ₹600 Cr (MF), plus PMS/AIF/insurance separately
- **Investors:** 600 – 2,500 families
- **ARN structure:** 2-5 ARNs (corporate + individual)
- **Geography:** Multi-city (HO + 2-4 branches)
- **Revenue mix:** 55% MF trail, 20% PMS/AIF commission, 10% insurance, 10% advisory fees, 5% loans against MF

### A day in their life
- Senior partner does HNI relationship; junior partner runs ops; staff handles transactions.
- Has *some* tech — usually an old CRM + Excel + RTA portal + a basic client app.
- Monthly partner meeting on AUM, top-10 client review, receivable reconciliation.
- Compliance officer manages BASL due diligence, audits, KYC refresh.

### Pain points
- Multi-ARN AUM consolidation is hell — every ARN reports separately.
- Trail commission reconciliation across ARNs leaks ~2-5% revenue annually (this number lands hard).
- PMS/AIF holdings sit outside MF systems — clients want one portfolio view.
- GST invoicing for advisory fees is a mess.
- Onboarding a new investor across multiple products takes 3-5 days.
- Compliance load is growing (BASL, SEBI advertising code, AMFI guidelines).

### What they say they want vs what they actually buy on
- **Says:** "We want consolidation and reporting."
- **Buys on:** Recovered revenue from reconciliation, audit-ready compliance, and being able to scale headcount without proportionally scaling chaos.

### Buying triggers
- New partner joins and pushes for systematisation.
- Audit finding on reconciliation gaps.
- A competing family firm in their city upgrades and starts winning their clients.
- Crossed ₹300 Cr AUM and operational drag is visible in P&L.

### Objections you will hear
- "We already have [existing CRM]. Why switch?"
- "Migration will disrupt our operations for months."
- "₹5L+ Enterprise is a big commitment."
- "Will you customise X, Y, Z for us?"
- "We need to see references from similar firms."

### Best-fit plan
**Enterprise — Custom (from ₹5 L)** — closing features:
1. **Multi-ARN AUM reconciliation + MF receivable** (the leaky revenue story — this is THE number)
2. **PMS/AIF/loans against MF — masters, txns, upload & download** (the consolidated view)
3. **Customisation — UI / workflow / reports + custom integrations** (the "fits how we already work" story)
4. **Dedicated Customer Success RM + BASL due diligence + GST invoicing** (the de-risking story)
5. **Broker contract note auto reading + Sanchay CRM integration** (the "we don't have to throw away what works" story)

### Disqualifiers
- Owner-operator who refuses to let go of their existing tool — sell to the next-generation partner instead.
- Sub-₹100 Cr AUM with no growth plan — they're a Pro+AI, not Enterprise.

## Persona 5 — "The National Distributor / Corporate ARN" (Top of the Stack)

### Snapshot
Corporate ARN, often a CA firm / NBFC / sub-broker that scaled into MF distribution. 20-100+ employees including a sales force of RMs. Multi-state. May have a parent broking/insurance entity.

### Book of business
- **AUM:** ₹500 Cr – ₹5,000 Cr+
- **Investors:** 2,000 – 50,000
- **ARN structure:** Corporate ARN + 5-50 employee sub-ARNs
- **Geography:** Multi-state, sometimes pan-India
- **Revenue mix:** Diversified — MF, PMS, AIF, insurance, lending, sometimes broking

### A day in their life
- CEO/MD-led. Has a CTO or tech head.
- Already runs a stack — sometimes home-grown, sometimes Wealthspectrum / Iris / FinX / NJ.
- RMs use a CRM; ops uses a back office; clients use an app — none stitched well.
- Board reporting, SEBI compliance, GST, TDS — all heavy.

### Pain points
- Existing stack is rigid, expensive to change, and the vendor's roadmap doesn't match theirs.
- Wants to build differentiated client experiences but the platform won't let them.
- RM productivity is plateauing — needs AI assist.
- Multi-asset client portfolio view across MF + PMS + AIF + insurance is still missing.
- Wants API access to build their own apps/journeys.

### What they say they want vs what they actually buy on
- **Says:** "We need a platform that scales with us and gives us APIs."
- **Buys on:** Switching cost vs incumbent (you have to make the migration painless), roadmap alignment, dedicated team, and a partnership posture, not a vendor posture.

### Buying triggers
- Incumbent vendor raises prices or fails to deliver a roadmap item.
- New CEO/COO mandate to consolidate stack.
- Funding round / acquisition / IPO prep — needs audit-ready, scalable tech.
- AUM crosses ₹1,000 Cr and back-office cracks become visible.

### Objections you will hear
- "We're already on [Wealthspectrum / Iris / NJ / FinX]."
- "Migration is too risky — what's your transition plan?"
- "We need uptime SLAs, data residency, security certifications."
- "Show me your roadmap for the next 18 months."
- "Can you sign an MSA with our legal team's redlines?"

### Best-fit plan
**Enterprise (well above ₹5 L starting)** — closing features:
1. **API Centre Access + custom integrations** (the "we'll build on top of you" story)
2. **Multi-ARN management + AUM reconciliation at scale** (the operations story)
3. **Dedicated Account Manager + Delivery Manager + Customer Success RM** (the partnership story)
4. **BASL due diligence + audit-ready compliance stack** (the de-risking story)
5. **Customisation across UI/workflow/reports** (the "we won't be locked into your opinion" story)
6. **Multi-asset marketplace — MF + PMS + AIF + loans against MF + fixed income + smallcase/stock baskets** (the one-platform story)

### Disqualifiers
- Already 2 years into a 5-year contract with an incumbent — circle back at renewal.
- No internal champion at C-level — Enterprise sales without one is a dead deal.
